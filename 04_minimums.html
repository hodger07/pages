Contract Minimums Reference

Both the EMC2 engine program and the P3 airframe program carry contractual annual minimums. These are obligations to Honda Aircraft and GE Honda regardless of actual usage.

Program Annual Minimum Current Rate Tony's Annual Share
EMC2 Engines 120 hrs/yr per engine $216.19/hr × 2 $25,944 (60 hr/per annum)
P3 Airframe - Hourly 120 flight hours/yr $332.25/hr $19,932 (60 hr/per annum)
P3 Airframe - Cycles 120 cycles/yr $288.87/cycle $17,328 (60 cycles/per annum)
P3 Airframe - Calendar Monthly (regardless) $1,568/mo $9,408
Total Annual Maintenance Minimums $72,612

If the aircraft flies fewer than 120 hours or 120 cycles in a calendar year, Honda Aircraft and GE Honda issue reconciliation invoices for the shortfall. Monthly billing of these reserves prevents that from becoming a lump-sum surprise at year-end.
