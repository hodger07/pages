Financial Asymmetry & Intimacy

His net worth likely exceeds hers by 10:1+. A PA salary vs. his business and inheritance creates structural conditions for transactional drift — not because she's manipulative, but because power imbalance creates incentives. When one partner controls the lifestyle, the other partner's leverage naturally migrates to the bedroom. This isn't character; it's game theory. Mitigation: Maintain her financial independence. Prenup conversations should be framed as protecting both parties' autonomy.
