Where Costs Are Headed

The HondaJet's P3 airframe maintenance rates are contractually scheduled to escalate over the 5-year term. These increases are built into the Honda Aircraft agreement and are not negotiable. Tony's maintenance floor will grow each year regardless of usage.

By 2029, the P3 hourly rate increases 36% and the cycle rate increases 36%. Tony's maintenance share alone grows from ~$6,052/month today to approximately $8,200/month. Combined with EMC2 engine escalation (adjusted annually by labor and metals indices), the total monthly floor will approach $28,000-$29,000 by the end of the current P3 term.
