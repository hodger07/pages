Volume Story

Demand grew ~31% across the network from 2023 to 2025 — from 7,839 to 10,261 jobs. That's the lead story. Katy drove the majority of volume growth (+1,240 jobs), while DFW was the fastest-growing territory on a percentage basis (+33%). Growth held even as the cancel problem emerged, which means the booking engine is healthy; the operational question is what's slipping through the funnel.

Territory Story

Katy and Spring are Houston's anchors — 77% of total volume — and they shared the same 2024 crisis and 2025 partial recovery. DFW is a different animal: it sailed through 2024 with a flat cancel rate while Houston territories were hemorrhaging, then quietly got worse in 2025. By year-end 2025, DFW's 23.4% cancel rate is the highest of the three territories and still climbing.

Warning Sign

The 2024 cancel spike was primarily behavioral — "Changed Mind - WHY?" exploded from 206 to 560 cases network-wide, a signal of weak sales and dispatch process discipline. That reason cratered back to 114 in 2025, suggesting real process improvement. But two new failure modes replaced it: "Service Not Offered" (168 cases, up from 17) and "Canceled via Text" (165 cases, up from 11). New leaks, different root causes — still unresolved heading into 2026.

What This Means & What to Do
01
DFW needs its own intervention — it did not share Houston's recovery.

Katy and Spring's 2024 cancel spike was behavioral ("Changed Mind") and showed real improvement in 2025. DFW never had that spike and never had that recovery. Its 2025 cancel rate of 23.4% is driven by ETA confirmation failures, service coverage gaps, and text cancels — process and coverage problems, not sales. Build a DFW-specific action plan, not a copy of whatever fixed Houston.

02
Audit "Service Not Offered" — 168 cases in 2025 didn't exist three years ago.

This reason went from essentially zero to the #4 cancellation driver network-wide. Either the service offering changed and the booking intake wasn't updated, or techs are arriving and declining jobs they were booked for. Both are fixable but they require different interventions. Find out which one it is before making assumptions.

03
Fix the text-cancel problem — 165 ghost cancels in 2025 are a dispatch and CRM failure.

"Canceled via Text" means a customer disengaged silently and the team couldn't get a reason. That's a follow-up and confirmation sequence gap. Implement automated confirmation at 24h and 2h pre-appointment with an explicit re-opt-in. Every silent cancel is a lost job you never even got to pitch.

04
Investigate CP_RepairElectrical — it's a statistical distortion hiding inside your cancel rate.

This job type cancels at 95-96% consistently across all three years and grew from 46 jobs in 2023 to 293 in 2025. If these are scheduling artifacts or workflow placeholders, they are artificially inflating the cancel rate for every territory. Until this is resolved, you don't have a clean cancel rate number to manage against.

05
Protect the panel replacement funnel — high-value jobs with a rising cancel rate are a margin risk.

Marketed lead panel replacements are likely among the highest-revenue job types, and their cancel rate jumped from 10% to 18.2% between 2023 and 2025 while volume contracted. Something is breaking between the marketing lead and the completed job. Map the drop-off points and plug the leak before 2026 marketing spend makes it worse.

06
Declare the 2024 "Changed Mind" crisis solved — and figure out exactly what fixed it.

"Changed Mind - WHY?" dropped from 560 cases in 2024 to 114 in 2025 — a 79% reduction. That is a genuine operational win. Something changed in the sales or dispatch process that caused it, and that change needs to be documented, owned, and protected. Don't let it regress while attention shifts to the new problems.
