The Angle Both Missed

🔴 State Franchise Law Violation

AB collected royalties for 7+ years without compliant FDD or renewal paper. In most franchise regulatory frameworks, this is a per se violation of state franchise disclosure laws — exposing AB to rescission, restitution, and statutory penalties. Not just from Rich — from every franchisee with a similar gap. This is the system-wide threat AB fears most.

🔴 Insurance Coverage Play

Does AB carry E&O, D&O, or franchise liability insurance covering the $5.7M exposure? Insurance carriers settle. A claim that triggers coverage creates pressure from AB's own risk infrastructure. CFAA/DTSA claims may fall under a separate cyber liability tower.

🔴 Albert/ABRH Entity Structure

Albert as sole manager/CEO of ABRH controls the operating entity that owns customer data, phone numbers, CRM, ServiceTitan tenant. The franchisor can't "flip a switch" when the franchisee's entity owns all operational infrastructure. This makes the replacement-cost argument even stronger.
