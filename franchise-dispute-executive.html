Here's Why the Demand Appears Inflated

Authority Brands' own documents — not ours — create the following problems with their demand:

  • 1
    Write-off contradicts the demand. On 2/28/2025, Authority Brands' own accounting team wrote off $97,738.84 on the Bustos account as "Bad Debt — Cleanup AR 2024." They then demand the larger balance 15 months later. Their own accounting records don't support their claim. See document ↗
  • 2
    2017 invoices are included that were already paid. The AB statement carries $31,134.97 from 2017 as currently outstanding. A Payment Receipt dated 3/27/2020 shows those exact invoices were paid in full. Action: Van needs to confirm the receipt maps to the specific invoices before this is used in writing. See document ↗
  • 3
    The $88,044 backbill was created retroactively and was never part of normal billing. Invoice INVMSPRK-29403 was created on 12/31/2024 as a single manual entry — never billed through the normal system. AB's own attorney (Lani Binnie) had no such amount in her August 2023 reconciliation. The 4-year limitations period bars everything before June 2022 regardless. Capture reconciliation ↗
  • 4
    Possible double-billing on South Denton territory. MS0156 (South Denton) closed in 2020. RGRH covered that territory and paid royalties to AB during the same period AB claims minimums were owed on MS0156. Action: The written documentation from Q3–Q4 2020 must be located and reviewed before this is asserted in any formal letter.
  • 5
    Incredible Electric: AB's own documents are $13,700 apart. AB's February 2026 addendum says $18,328. Sturm's May 2026 letter says $4,628. Same entity, same AB, three months apart, no explanation. A creditor that can't state a consistent number across its own records struggles to prove any specific amount. See statement ↗
  • 6
    RGRH: The originating invoice was paid in full. Invoice INVMSPRK-26639 shows PAID $5,520.19, balance $0.00. AB's own Revenue Manager Morgan Graham wrote in December 2023 that both accounts "will be caught up by Capture 24." AB never produced that reconciliation. See invoice ↗

The Most Important Thing to Understand About the Renewal Addendum

The dollar amounts are not the biggest risk. The Exhibit C General Release is. Signing the Renewal Addendum as written would require releasing Authority Brands from "any and all suits, claims... from the beginning of time to the Effective Date, known or unknown." That language would extinguish any claims related to billing practices, system access (ServiceTitan/CFAA), and bad-faith dealing — before those claims can ever be asserted. The renewal documents also require full payment of all disputed receivables within 5 business days or the right to dispute is forfeited. Do not sign any renewal document without specific carve-outs negotiated by counsel.

Here's What Needs to Happen

  • Do not sign any renewal, addendum, or release that bundles disputed receivables into franchise renewal rights without counsel approval and explicit carve-outs.
  • Van — confirm the 3/27/2020 payment receipt maps to Invoice series SI000000818–SI000001433 specifically.
  • Locate the Q3–Q4 2020 written documentation confirming RGRH covered South Denton territory and paid royalties to AB on that revenue.
  • Rob Salkowski has a draft response letter ready to send to Sturm once these two documents are confirmed.
  • Do not pay any amount under the current demand without counsel review — the stated demand does not appear supported by AB's own records.

Two Items Required Before Sending the Response Letter

  • Receipt mapping: Van confirms 3/27/2020 receipt applies to SI000000818–SI000001433 invoice series. This closes the paid-balance argument cleanly.
  • MS0156 documentation: Written proof from Q3–Q4 2020 that RGRH serviced South Denton and paid royalties on that revenue. This is the double-billing argument. Without the document, it's an assertion, not evidence.

The response letter should not go out until both items are confirmed.
