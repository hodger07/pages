Three Issues with This Statement

Issue 1 — No Payments Shown (Payment Column Blank)
AB's statement shows zero payments received against this account since 1/1/2019. Yet a Payment Receipt dated 3/27/2020 confirms $31,134.97 was paid in full for the 2017 invoices comprising the Balance Forward. Normal capture payments through 2022 (all confirmed paid per Open Invoices Part 1 & 2) also do not appear. This is not a complete transaction history — it is a selective presentation of charges without corresponding credits.
Issue 2 — Balance Forward Represents Already-Paid Invoices
The $31,134.97 Balance Forward carried from 1/1/2019 represents 11 invoices from March–August 2017. These were paid in full on 3/27/2020 — payment receipt on file, applied amount confirmed. AB is re-asserting invoices for which it has already received and acknowledged payment. This $31,134.97 must be excluded from any legitimate demand.
Issue 3 — Unexplained Internal Journal Entries (~$22,000+)
The statement contains multiple "Journal #JEBF" and "Journal #JEMSPRK" entries totaling over $22,000 that are not normal capture invoices. These are internal AB accounting adjustments. No supporting documentation has been produced for what these entries represent, when the underlying obligation arose, or how they were calculated. Until AB produces documentation for each journal entry, these amounts cannot be treated as liquidated claims.
