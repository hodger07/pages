Section 3: The Section 2 Trap

Walk through what happens after you sign:

Step 1: You sign the extension + release.
Step 2: All your claims and defenses are gone.
Step 3: Within 30 days, AB sends invoices for disputed amounts ($200K+).
Step 4: You have 30 days to pay or dispute.
Step 5: You dispute — because they can't document the $200K. We already know this from March.
Step 6: "Meet and confer" — no binding resolution mechanism.
Step 7: Dispute remains unresolved.
Step 8: AB invokes "default and termination provisions in the applicable Franchise Agreements."
Step 9: AB terminates 14 franchise agreements.
Step 10: You have no counterclaims, no defenses, no offset — you released everything.

Rob said receivables would be separate. Sturm embedded them with a termination trigger. This is the trap.
