An AI-first field service management platform for HVAC and electrical companies in Texas, built to replace ServiceTitan. Not a cheaper SaaS clone — a continuous intelligence loop ServiceTitan structurally cannot deliver. This document is the minimum build that gets JFI off ServiceTitan with AI at the center.

Executive Summary

JFI replaces ServiceTitan with a platform where every call, visit, and review feeds an AI loop that scores performance and coaches the business toward higher margins. The spine is a Laravel monolith for business logic and a Python/FastAPI AI sidecar for all ML, joined by a Redis Streams event bus.

This is an MVP-only plan. Seven module areas kill ServiceTitan on Day 1. Everything that can wait has been cut. Oct 2026 AI tooling (Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT-5.4, Cursor agentic mode) materially lowers the build cost versus the Feb 2026 v2 estimate — the MVP drops from 1,808 hrs to roughly 1,235 hrs.

Timeline: 5–6 months with an 8-person team at ~$100K/month burn. $650K already spent (sunk). New cash needed to reach MVP: ~$400K–$675K.

Top 5 Risks

1. Multi-tenant design failureCRITICAL
Senior architect must own tenancy model end-to-end. Spike in Week 1 before anything else is built on it.
2. Offline-first complexity (Tech Mobile)CRITICAL
WatermelonDB or PowerSync. Dedicated architecture spike in Week 2 to lock the sync strategy.
3. Design-to-code gap exceeds $108K–$144KHIGH
Validate the full Figma surface area before committing the budget. The gap is real labor, not a rounding error.
4. ServiceTitan data migrationHIGH
Build the adapter layer early. Dual-write during transition so neither system is the single source of failure.
5. AI model accuracyHIGH
Rule-based fallbacks for every ML feature. Human approval gates on all auto-pricing — no silent margin changes.

Break-Even & ROI

ServiceTitan costs $80K–$150K/yr. Custom platform maintenance post-build runs $120K–$180K/yr.

You do not build this to save on SaaS fees. You build it because ServiceTitan cannot do the continuous intelligence loop.

ROI lands in Year 2–3, when AI coaching lifts conversion 5–10% and margins 3–5%. That is where the investment pays back — not on the subscription line.
