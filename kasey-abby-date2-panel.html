If the route is long, the pace is aggressive, or he's clearly optimizing for performance instead of connection — she's a training partner, not a date. The run has to feel like a date that happens to be a run, not a run that he's being polite about.

Risk 3: Timing and logistics. A run requires more coordination than a walk. Time of day, distance, pace, where to meet, what to do after. If the logistics feel clunky or he seems uncertain about any of it — the boldness of the invite gets undermined by the execution.

How to mitigate all three: Know the route before he sends the text. Keep it to 3-5 miles. Suggest morning. Have a coffee or food plan for after. The invite should feel spontaneous; the plan should be clean.
