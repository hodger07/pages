Kasey + Abby — Operating System A / Operating System B

Built from Kasey's canonical profile (Birkman, behavioral patterns, dating history, ibogaine integration) and Abby's full dossier (family fracture, PA training, communication style, physical/emotional progression). Each person gets their own parts map before the couple system is mapped.

System A = wound-managed / have-to fuel
System B = enough / clear / chosen
Parts maps are derived from their actual data, not projected from Rich's framework

Kasey Hodge

27. Partner & Operations Director. Private pilot. Eagle Scout. No degree, no debt, no alcohol in 2026. Ibogaine-cracked-open 13 months ago. Birkman Assertiveness gap of 58 (shows soft, needs strong push). Searching for a wife. Done with casual sex by choice.

Kasey's Parts Map

The King (Self)

Who Kasey is when he's grounded: warm, emotionally brave, competent, direct without being aggressive, willing to be seen. Post-ibogaine Kasey with heart open. The version of him that brought up divorce on Date 4 and said "there's no rush" about sex. Leads from clarity, not hunger.

Little Kasey (Exile)

The wound: a kid who grew up watching his mom battle ALS, lost Michael, lost Paige, and carries grief tattoos on his body. The part that desperately wants to be chosen by a specific woman and fears being too much for her. "I was worried it would be too much for girls." That line is Little Kasey talking.

The Soft Front (Manager) — Assertiveness 23

Shows up accommodating, easy, gives people outs. Doesn't push. This is the Birkman gap in action: presents at 23 but needs 81. Without external push (Rich, life pressure, a strong woman), Kasey can plateau, drift, or let things die that should have been fought for. In dating: gives easy outs to women, doesn't escalate when he should, waits instead of moves. In the relationship: risks becoming passive if Abby's caretaker takes over.

Dick (Firefighter — Push Mode)

When Little Kasey gets activated: over-pursuit. More texts, more plans, more proving, more intensity. In dating history this looked like the womanizer phase — conquest as reassurance. In this relationship: could show up as flooding Abby during the 10-day trip, future-casting too fast, or trying to lock things down before the foundation is fully built. Pursuit energy that feels like pressure to a woman who paces carefully.

Tom (Firefighter — Withdraw Mode)

The other response when the wound activates: full disappear. Goes quiet, gets cool, creates distance before he can be rejected. In dating: the slow-roll with Natalia after Date 3 — just let the texting die instead of saying it directly. In this relationship: if Abby's text tone goes flat or she pulls back to regulate, Tom could grab the wheel and create a cold standoff neither of them intended.

Harry (Manager — System Builder)

The part that tries to engineer outcomes. In career: built real operational systems, runs 30+ trucks. In dating: the Hinge optimization, the LinkedIn strategy, the dating panel. Useful when channeled into structure. Dangerous when it starts trying to strategize the relationship itself — turning Abby into a project to be managed rather than a person to be known.

System A Kasey

Little Kasey gets activated. The Soft Front gives too much room. Dick or Tom takes the wheel.

  • Reads Abby's flat texts or slow replies as early signs of abandonment
  • Relief at being seen gets mistaken for "this is definitely the one"
  • Dick floods: more texts, more plans, more emotional intensity than the moment calls for
  • Tom withdraws: goes quiet, gets cool, creates distance to pre-empt rejection
  • Harry over-strategizes: turns the relationship into a project with tactics instead of trust
  • The Soft Front accommodates her pacing without ever naming what he actually needs

System B Kasey

The King drives. Little Kasey is heard but not obeyed. The Soft Front becomes genuine warmth instead of accommodation.

  • Enough: Abby is a gift, not medicine for the wound
  • Here: a flat text is a flat text, not proof of loss
  • Curious: "what is actually happening?" instead of "what does this mean about me?"
  • Clear: names what he needs directly — "I notice I lose you over text, that matters to me"
  • Grateful: receives her care without fusing his identity to it
  • Chooses: lets consistency earn deeper access instead of rushing the timeline

Where System A breaks Kasey

Flat texts. Slow replies. Abby regulating by going controlled or formal. The 10-day absence. Any moment where the warmth dims and Little Kasey reads it as "she's leaving." Also: being so accommodating (Assertiveness 23) that he never names his own needs until resentment builds.

Where System B accelerates

Keeping his own life full (work, running, Blu, mission). Naming fear without obeying it. Letting her text style be her problem, not his wound. Being direct when something matters. Staying warm without chasing. Trusting that reliability over time proves more than intensity in the moment.

Abby Frank

25. Trauma PA (BCM). Parents divorced at 3. Bio parents don't speak. Dad distant. Stepmom not close. Built safety with siblings. Mothered her younger half-brother. 4-year D1 softball player. Funniest Teammate award. Started going to church on her own. No boyfriend since 2022. Hates texting. Warm, nurturing, physically affectionate, and deeply present in person.

Abby's Parts Map

Little Abby (Exile)

The 3-year-old whose parents split and stopped speaking. The kid who was not fought over but divided. Dad became distant. Stepmom never really landed. The wound is not dramatic abandonment — it's quiet disappearance. Adults who were supposed to be there just… weren't. Safety had to be built sideways, with siblings, with her own hands.

The Nurse (Manager — Caretaker)

The part that stepped up when the adults failed. Mothered her half-brother by choice. Feeds people. Packs lunches. Remembers the cookies. Cooks dinner. Handles grief without flinching. This part earns love by being indispensable. It is genuine warmth, but it is also a survival strategy: if I am useful enough, I am safe enough. Risk: she gives until she's empty, then quietly resents without ever having asked for what she needed.

The Chart (Manager — Clinical Container)

Strips communication down to clean, efficient, low-risk signal. Corporate memo texting. SBAR-style handoffs bleeding into personal life. This part protects Little Abby from being overexposed, misread, or trapped by permanent written record of wanting something. In person she has tone, touch, timing, repair. Over text she has none of that, so The Chart takes over and keeps it sterile.

The Gate (Firefighter — Threshold Control)

Controls the final step of sexual and emotional surrender. High desire with a deliberate brake. She can initiate kissing, get on top, orgasm, stay close — but The Gate holds the line at full sex and full reciprocity until safety is deeply verified. She named it herself: "I have a problem transitioning into sex with new people." That is The Gate talking — not lack of desire, but the need to control the crossing point.

The Wall (Firefighter — Distance Mode)

When she feels pressured, misread over text, or forced to receive care she didn't ask for — The Wall goes up. Goes quiet. Goes formal. Goes clinical. This is not hostility. It is self-protection. The Wall looks like the corporate memo, the controlled pacing, the slight coolness after a big night. It is what happens when Little Abby's system detects that the environment might not be safe enough to stay open.

System A Abby

The Nurse runs the show. The Chart handles all mediated communication. The Gate controls the sexual threshold. Little Abby stays hidden.

  • Nurtures instead of receiving — earns love through usefulness
  • Texts like a memo because written vulnerability feels dangerous
  • Paces everything carefully — not from wisdom but from fear of losing control
  • Desire is real but final surrender stays locked behind The Gate
  • Anger or hurt turns into The Wall: quiet, formal, distant
  • Can become indispensable to Kasey without ever letting him see Little Abby

System B Abby

Little Abby is safe enough to be seen. The Nurse offers care freely, not as a transaction. The Chart loosens. The Gate opens on her timeline, not from fear.

  • Enough: she does not have to earn love by being useful
  • Here: stays in the room instead of retreating into controlled channels
  • Curious: notices her own defenses without defending them
  • Clear: can say "I'm scared" or "I'm angry" without packaging it
  • Grateful: receives care without apologizing or redirecting
  • Chooses: lets trust deepen because she wants to, not because she verified enough safety metrics

Where System A breaks Abby

Being pressured to text warmly when she can't. Conflict that doesn't get repaired in person. Having to receive care (activates The Wall because receiving feels like losing control). Being asked to disclose more than she's ready to. Kasey's pursuit energy (Dick) feeling like demand instead of desire.

Where System B accelerates

Kasey being predictably present without being intense. Being specifically noticed for what she does ("you packed my lunch — that meant something"). No-rush pacing that lets The Gate open naturally. Safe in-person conversations where she can use her real tools (tone, touch, presence). Kasey naming something honestly without making her responsible for fixing his feeling about it.

The Couple System

Where his parts meet her parts. The relationship doesn't break because chemistry is weak. It breaks if his wound meets her control and both mistake the collision for the truth about each other.

System A Couple — Where It Breaks

  • Abby's Chart sends a flat text. Kasey's Little Kasey reads it as withdrawal. Dick floods or Tom goes cold. Abby's Wall goes up in response. Spiral.
  • The Nurse over-functions. The Soft Front accommodates. Neither names what they actually need. Both feel unseen while looking close.
  • The Gate stays locked because Dick's pursuit energy feels like pressure, not patience.
  • Kasey mistakes relief (finally being seen) for destiny (she's the answer). Abby mistakes caregiving (being useful) for intimacy (being known).
  • Conflict gets managed by The Nurse and avoided by The Soft Front. Nothing real gets said. Resentment accumulates in clean packaging.

System B Couple — Where It Accelerates

  • Kasey stays warm without chasing. Abby stays open without over-managing. Both nervous systems learn that the other person is still there even when the signal dims.
  • Kasey names the pattern directly: "I notice I get in my head when your texts go short. That's mine, but I wanted you to know." Abby receives it without The Wall.
  • Abby lets Kasey carry something real for her — and doesn't redirect, minimize, or caretake her way out of receiving it.
  • The Gate opens because The King's patience earned it, not because Dick's pursuit pressured it.
  • In-person contact becomes the place where ruptures repair, not just where chemistry happens. Text loses its danger because the live bond proves itself over and over.
  • Admiration, softness, steadiness, honesty, and genuine knowing compound instead of novelty doing all the lifting.
The hinge: This does not become a great relationship because the chemistry gets hotter or because they "align" on enough values. It becomes great if Kasey stops treating every signal change as proof of the old wound, Abby stops treating every vulnerability gap as a management problem, and both of them learn to stay in the room when the room stops being easy — not because they have to, but because they are enough, and they choose to.
