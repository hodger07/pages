Annual Programs Contract Minimums Reference

Both the EMC2 engine program and the P3 airframe program carry contractual annual minimums of 120 hours and 120 cycles. These are obligations to Honda Aircraft and GE Honda regardless of actual usage. The partnership allocates 60 hours per partner, billed monthly regardless of usage. If a partner gets ahead of their utilization pacing, the monthly program billing for that partner will suspend until utilization falls back below their monthly YTD targets.

Program Annual Minimum Current Rate Tony's Annual Share
EMC2 Engines 120 hrs/yr per engine $216.19/hr × 2 $25,944 ($2,162/mo)
P3 Airframe - Hourly 120 flight hours/yr $332.25/hr $19,932 ($1,661/mo)
P3 Airframe - Cycles 120 cycles/yr $288.87/cycle $17,328 ($1,444/mo)
P3 Airframe - Calendar Monthly (regardless) $1,568/mo $9,408 ($784/mo)
Total Annual Maintenance Minimums $72,612 ($6,051/mo)

If the aircraft flies fewer than 120 hours or 120 cycles in a calendar year, Honda Aircraft and GE Honda issue reconciliation invoices for the shortfall. Monthly billing of these reserves prevents that from becoming a lump-sum surprise at year-end.

How the 5-hour monthly minimum works. Each partner pays for 5 flight hours and 5 cycles every month regardless of whether they fly. Unused hours and cycles carry forward through the year - so if you don't fly in January, that capacity is still available in later months, but the cost is already committed. The only way maintenance costs go beyond the numbers shown above is if a partner exceeds their 60 hours or 60 cycles per year. Beyond that, the only additional costs are fuel and travel expenses for each trip.
