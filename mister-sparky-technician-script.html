Technician Call Script — V4
12 phases. One call. One shot.
Short phases are below. Longer phases link to their own page — tap the link to go deep.
All technician dialogue is in this font so you always know what to say.
1
Pre-Arrival
  • Mental End Zone Preparation — get your head right
  • Vehicle clean, shiny tires, organized, presentable
  • Phone and iPad on vibrate
  • Leave all problems in the van
  • You must look and act the part
You are not a fixer. You are a professional. The call starts before you touch the door.
2
Approaching the Home
  • Walk with confidence, good posture, SMILE
  • Fast brisk pace from van to door
  • Do NOT walk through grass or flower beds
  • Minimum required tools only — a doctor doesn't carry all his tools

At the Door

  • Knock on the door frame
  • Step back, 45-degree angle to the door
  • Tool bag on the ground
  • SMILE
3
Introduction & Entry
Technician"Hi, my name is _______, I am with Mister Sparky! I'm here to see Mr/Mrs/Ms. Doe."
Technician"Good to meet you, Mr. [Sir name]. I'm going to be your electrician today!"
Technician"Do you mind if I call you Bob?"

Van Placement

Technician"Is my van parked okay, Bob?"
Technician"Great, I just want to make sure I am not going to block any parking for family, friends, yard crews, or deliveries."

Entry & Floor Savers

Technician"Do you mind if I come in?"
Technician"Let me put on my floor savers."

Why You're There

Technician"I understand that you have a few outlets not working."
Don't lead with the problem unless the customer forces the issue. Roll with it if they do, then circle back to rapport.
Technician"No problem at all, you don't need to worry Bob — we will get that problem fixed up for you and get you back to normal."
4
Rapport Building (F.O.R.M.)
Full Script →
This is the most important phase. If you skip this, the rest of the script won't work. Build a REAL connection — not a checkbox.
  • Family — Occupation — Recreation — Material Possession
  • This should NOT be pre-scripted — it needs to flow naturally
  • Move on when they're giving real answers, not just "yes/no"
  • Grade yourself: A (activated + connected) through D (acted like a hammer)
5
Setting Critical Anchors
Full Script →
Two anchors. Both must be locked verbally before you move on. The math does the selling — you just hold the calculator.
  • Anchor 1: They agree to the 4% home maintenance rule
  • Anchor 2: They agree to 20-30% of deferred maintenance = electrical
  • Use a real calculator — NOT your phone
  • Goal: "We both agree you don't want to touch that number today"
6
Lock In the Decision Maker
Technician"Bob, based on the fact that you said you and your wife really don't have a strategy for managing your home's maintenance, I don't want to assume who takes the lead on making these type of decisions or if both of you are involved once it reaches a certain dollar amount — but I need to make sure that all parties are available for time and efficiency purposes during the call today."
Pause — wait for them to name spouse, themselves, or both.
Technician"Got it — I like to have everyone who's part of the decision in the loop, so no one feels out of the loop or surprised later. If we do find something worth addressing today, would it be possible for both of you to be available for that part of the conversation?"
Get this commitment NOW. If you skip this, you'll hear "I need to talk to my spouse" at the close and it's 10x harder.
7
Problem Discovery & Pre-Inspection
Full Script →
Find the EMOTIONAL driver — not just the technical problem. "I can't bake and that's my hobby" is worth 10x more than "outlet doesn't work."
  • Ask when it started, what happened, has it happened before
  • Show compassion — acknowledge it's frustrating
  • Deliver credibility: "[X] years experience, you're in good hands"
  • "My goal today is to earn the right to be your electrician for as long as you own the home"
  • ACTIVATE against their emotional drivers and personal goals
8
Presenting the Diagnostic
Full Script →
You're not "selling a diagnostic" — you're explaining WHY circuit-level troubleshooting is the only way to actually solve the problem and prevent it from returning.
  • Sit down with them — ask for a comfortable spot
  • Lead with good news: panel looks solid
  • Be upfront: "I wasn't able to isolate the root cause yet — that's normal"
  • Educate: home = one big system made of smaller circuits
  • Two reasons: "So I can look you in the eye" + "help ensure it doesn't happen again"
9
Teeing Up the Circuit Restoration
Full Script →
The customer should talk themselves into the restoration. You ask the questions — they arrive at the conclusion. Never answer your own question.
  • Show them the fix — reconnect to their emotional driver
  • "All the devices are wired the same way" — let THEM say it'll happen again
  • "What do you think we'd have to do again?" — let THEM say "diagnose it again"
  • Soft pitch: credit diagnostic fee toward full solution
  • DON'T SPEAK until they answer
10
Presenting the Options
Full Script →
Three options. Start from the top. Let the silence do the work at the close. The Costco analogy makes "Best" feel logical, not expensive.
  • Best = most comprehensive, two circuits, future-proofed (Costco analogy)
  • Better = one circuit + safety, diagnostic fee rolled in
  • Essentials = baseline safety only, no restoration
  • "Which option feels right for you today?" — then DO NOT SPEAK
11
Objection Handling
Full Script →
45%+ of people are High Stability. "I need to talk to my spouse" is usually a smokescreen for "I think the price is too high." Your job is to discover which one it is.
  • Spouse objection: Discover if it's real or a smokescreen
  • "Think about it": Systematically eliminate concerns until you find the real one
  • Know the 4 personality types: Driver, Influencer, Stable, Compliant
  • If real condition: get spouse on phone or set callback appointment
12
Closing Techniques
Full Script →
Break it down to the ridiculous. $800 over 7 years = $0.31/day. Then give them an easy choice: "Credit card or our money?" Not "do you want to buy this?"
  • Reduction Close: Break the gap down to cents per day
  • Options Close: Show a lesser-priced item
  • Service Agreement Close: Discount via Just Fix It App
  • Easy Choice Close: "Card or our money?" — not a yes/no on the total
