Phase 8

Decision Maker Alignment

Two calm control points before price
🎯 Phase Goal Confirm who is part of the decision before options. This is not a trap — it is an anchor you will tie back to if a spouse objection appears later.
Decision Maker Script First touch: early agenda
Technician"Bob, earlier I asked who else needs to be part of the conversation if we found something meaningful. Before I walk you through options, I want to make sure I’m not leaving anyone out. Is anyone else who helps make this decision available now?"
📝 NotePause. Let them tell you whether the decision is theirs, their spouse's, or shared. Do not argue. Anchor it.
Technician"Got it. I like to have everyone who’s part of the decision in the loop, because I don’t want anyone surprised later. If they are available, let’s bring them in now. If not, I’ll make sure we set a specific time to review this together."
Why This Matters Avoid late-stage friction
  • It respects the household's real decision process.
  • It prevents a surprise spouse objection at the end of the call.
  • It keeps the technician from presenting options to the wrong audience.
  • It makes larger recommendations feel transparent instead of pressured.
⚠ ImportantDo this as alignment, not control. The tone is: “I want the right people informed,” not “I need to trap everyone in the room.”
If the Other Decision Maker Is Not Available Keep it consultative
Technician"No problem. If we uncover something simple, we can handle it today. If we uncover something bigger, I'll make sure you have clear options and enough information to review it together without either of you feeling rushed."
🔑 Key TakeawayThe goal is not to force a same-day decision. The goal is to keep the customer informed, reduce surprise, and preserve trust.
