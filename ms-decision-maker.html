Phase 6

Lock In the Decision Maker

Get the right people involved before recommendations or price
🎯 Phase Goal Confirm who is part of the decision early, respectfully, and without making the customer feel cornered. This protects the customer, the technician, and the close.
Decision Maker Script Use before discovery
Technician"Bob, based on the fact that you said you and your wife really don't have a strategy for managing your home's maintenance, I don't want to assume who takes the lead on making these types of decisions or if both of you are involved once it reaches a certain dollar amount — but I need to make sure that all parties are available for time and efficiency purposes during the call today."
📝 NotePause. Let them tell you whether the decision is theirs, their spouse's, or shared. Do not argue with the answer.
Technician"Got it — I like to have everyone who's part of the decision in the loop, so no one feels out of the loop or surprised later. If we do find something worth addressing today, would it be possible for both of you to be available for that part of the conversation?"
Why This Matters Avoid late-stage friction
  • It respects the household's real decision process.
  • It prevents a surprise spouse objection at the end of the call.
  • It keeps the technician from presenting options to the wrong audience.
  • It makes larger recommendations feel transparent instead of pressured.
⚠ ImportantDo this as alignment, not control. The tone is: “I want the right people informed,” not “I need to trap everyone in the room.”
If the Other Decision Maker Is Not Available Keep it consultative
Technician"No problem. If we uncover something simple, we can handle it today. If we uncover something bigger, I'll make sure you have clear options and enough information to review it together without either of you feeling rushed."
🔑 Key TakeawayThe goal is not to force a same-day decision. The goal is to keep the customer informed, reduce surprise, and preserve trust.
