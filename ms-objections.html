Phases 11 & 12

Objection Handling & Closing

The Four Personality Types

Driver

Direct, straightforward objections

Influencer

Sarcastic objections

Stable

Avoids conflict — uses smokescreens

Compliant

Asks detailed questions

45%+ of the population falls into High Stability. The "I need to talk to my spouse" objection often comes from this type — it's frequently a smokescreen, not a real condition. You must master handling this objection.

"I Need to Talk to My Spouse"

When to address it: BEFORE it occurs! If you observe the client is a High S personality and constantly bringing up their spouse, that means:

  • She has not reached a comfort level with you — work on the relationship!
  • You are probably going to hear the spouse objection

Rule: When repairs exceed single-spouse approval, you need the involvement of the other spouse.

Discover If It's a Smokescreen

Bob"I need to talk to my spouse."
Technician"That makes a lot of sense Bob. Do you think she will have some questions about what we covered today?"
Bob"Yes I do."
Technician"What type of questions do you think she will ask?"
Bob"She'll probably ask about the price."
Technician"Well, that's an easy one to answer. Do you think she will ask about the technical side of the installation process?"
Bob"I don't think so. Probably not, she is more concerned about price."
Technician"Oh. Well what do you think she will say about that price?"
Bob"It's too high."
Technician"I bet that is exactly what she'll say. Bob, what do you say about the price?"
Bob"Well, uhm. This is a little awkward. Uh, I think the price is a little high."
You have just discovered that the spouse is probably NOT the issue. It's the client's perception of your value and price. Continue to address the price objection.

If It's a Real Condition (Not a Smokescreen)

You have 2 choices:

  • Option A: Get the spouse involved over the phone — they keep the diagnostic credit
  • Option B: Set an appointment to come back and visit together
You can only roll the diagnostic into the circuit restoration if you do it now. If you must come back, you lose the efficiency, and they lose the savings. Doing it now is a win-win.
Technician"Absolutely — I'd want to do the same thing. This is an investment in your home, and it's worth making together. Just so we don't lose momentum and so your spouse has the same info you do, would it be possible to give them a quick call or put them on speaker? Sometimes it helps if I'm here to answer any questions in real time instead of you having to re-explain later."

"I Want to Think About It"

Technician"That's fine, Bob, I understand. This is an important choice for you. You wouldn't take your time thinking it over unless you were seriously interested, would you?"
Bob"That's right. I'm interested; I just need some time to think it over."

Clarify the Truth

Technician"Just so that I have it clear in my mind, you do feel that your home needs the options I have shown you, don't you?"
Bob"Yes I do. You have proven that very clearly."
Technician"Oh good, because I was worried that I offended you, and you were trying to kindly get rid of me. That's not the case, is it?"
Bob"No, No, No. I like you!"
Technician"Oh good, because the last thing I would want to do is offend you!"

Check Off the List

Technician"Since it is my job to answer all of your concerns, will you tell me what it is you were wanting to think over?"

Then systematically eliminate:

  • "Was it the options I recommended?"
  • "Is the quality of my work a concern?"
  • "What about the reputation of our company?"
  • "What about the guarantees and warranties?"
  • Reconfirm other value points…
Technician"Ok Bob. Level with me — is it the investment that is causing your concerns?"
Bob"That's a big part of it of course. It's just a lot of money."
You've now isolated the real objection: price. Everything else was noise. Now you can address it directly with a closing technique.

Closing Techniques

Technician"You probably have a number in your head that you were thinking this was going to be. Right?"
Bob"Well, sorta."
Technician"Most people do. And I bet, if that number you are thinking about is the same one on the page, you would be doing the paperwork right now, is that true?"
Bob"Yes."
Technician"OK Bob. Then, can you tell me how much, too much, you feel it is?"
Bob"It's about $800 more than what I was expecting."

Available Closes

The Options Close — show an item of lesser price
The Service Agreement Close — discount via Just Fix It App Download and Fix More Save More
The Reduction Close — break it down to the ridiculous

The Reduction Close (Example)

Technician"So, really, we are talking about $800, aren't we?"
Technician"All right. I think we should look closely at the total over the life of your electrical system. Here."
Hand Bob the calculator.
Technician"You said you plan on living in the home for at least the next 7 years, right?"
Technician"Our Circuit Restoration has a minimum of 7 years of worry-free service with no repairs, that's guaranteed here in writing."
$800 ÷ 7 years = $114/year
$114 ÷ 365 days = $0.31/day
Technician"The peace of mind alone will pay for that minor daily difference. Let me ask you one last thing."
Technician"Won't this Circuit Restoration, with: the peace of mind, low maintenance, zero repairs for 7 years, our great company to stand behind the installation, and all of our in-writing assurance guarantees be worth more than $0.31 a day of your time?"
Bob"Yes, I think it will."
Technician"Great! Then we are on the same page, aren't we?"
Bob"Yes…"

The Easy Choice Close

Technician"By the way, did you want to put this on a credit card you get points on or did you prefer to use our money?"
This is the Easy Choice Close. It is a whole lot easier to decide on a simple thing like "Card or my money" than deciding on the grand total of the install. You're giving them a simple A/B choice — not a yes/no on the full amount.
You never asked "do you want to buy this?" You asked "how do you want to pay?" The decision was already made — you're just helping them execute it.
