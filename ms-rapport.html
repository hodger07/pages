Phase 4
Rapport Building
F.O.R.M. — Family · Occupation · Recreation · Material Possession
Opening the Conversation
Technician

"Bob, since this is the first time we have worked together — would it be okay if we sat down for a few minutes to review everything where you are most comfortable in the house before I get started?"

⚠ Important
This should not be pre-scripted. It needs to flow from you naturally. If it sounds rehearsed, it won't work. Make it genuine — you're asking because you actually want to understand them.
The F.O.R.M. Framework

These four areas are your roadmap for building real connection. Work through them naturally — you don't have to hit them all in order.

F
Family

Who are they? Spouse, kids, pets? What does their home life look like? What matters most to them at home?

O
Occupation

What do they do for a living? Are they proud of it? Does it explain their priorities, their schedule, their financial mindset?

R
Recreation

What do they love doing? Hobbies, passions, weekend activities? This is often where emotional drivers live.

M
Material Possession

What do they love about their home? Recent upgrades? Things they're proud of? This tells you how they value their space.

Transition Into F.O.R.M.
Technician

"Before we get started, can I ask you a few questions just to help me better serve you as a client?"

  • "If you don't mind, tell me about yourself and your family?"
  • "What do you enjoy doing the most in your life?"
  • "What drives you and makes you tick?"
  • "What do you enjoy the most and least of home ownership?"
📝 Note
These are conversation starters, not an interrogation. Listen actively. Follow threads. Let them talk. The goal is to find what activates them — what truly drives them.
When to Move On
  • They're providing good information beyond yes/no answers
  • You feel a genuine connection forming
  • They seem comfortable, open, and ready to engage
  • You've identified at least one emotional driver
🔑 Key Takeaway
"Activate" means you've connected with what truly drives them in life — not just surface-level small talk. Until you hit that, keep going. An activated customer is a customer who trusts you.
Rapport Scorecard

After every visit, grade yourself honestly. This is how you get better.

A
Activated & Connected — Connected with what drives them AND personally/professionally. Full rapport. The gold standard.
B
Activated Only — Found their emotional driver but didn't build the personal/professional connection. Still strong, but incomplete.
C
Asked F.O.R.M. — Went through the motions. Asked the questions but didn't activate or truly connect. Mechanical, not human.
D
No Rapport Built — Acted as a tool, not a human. Showed up, diagnosed, quoted. Zero connection. This is a problem.
⚠ Important
Grade B or below means you're leaving money on the table — and more importantly, leaving a person feeling unheard. Aim for A every single visit.
