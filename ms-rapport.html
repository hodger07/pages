Phase 4

Structured Relationship & Control

Four questions. Four anchors. Use them later.
🎯 Phase Goal Build a real connection with guardrails. These are electricians, not therapists. Ask four structured questions, capture the customer's words, and use those words later to keep control.
Opening the Conversation Set the agenda without making it weird
Opening Script
Technician

"Before we get started, can I ask you a few questions just to help me better serve you as a client?"

⚠ ImportantDo not freestyle. Ask the questions one at a time, listen, and write down the exact words they use. The goal is not small talk — the goal is anchors.
The F.O.R.M. Framework Your roadmap for building real connection

These four areas create the human anchors you will use later. The best techs can make it feel natural; the script gives average techs rails.

F
Family

Who are they? Spouse, kids, pets? What does their home life look like? What matters most to them at home?

O
Occupation

What do they do for a living? Does it explain their priorities, their schedule, their financial mindset?

R
Recreation

What do they love doing? Hobbies, passions, weekend activities? This is often where emotional drivers live.

M
Material Possession

What do they love about their home? Recent upgrades? Things they're proud of? This tells you how they value their space.

Transition Into F.O.R.M. Questions to get them talking
Setup Script
Technician

"Before we get started, can I ask you a few questions just to help me better serve you as a client?"

Question Bank
  • "If you don't mind, tell me about yourself and your family?"
    Why: identifies who the home protects, who uses the space, and who may influence the decision.
  • "What do you enjoy doing the most in your life?"
    Why: gives you a human anchor to reconnect the repair to something they actually care about.
  • "What drives you and makes you tick?"
    Why: reveals values — safety, order, comfort, independence, pride, family, peace of mind. These become leverage later.
  • "What do you enjoy the most and least about home ownership?"
    Why: bridges rapport into maintenance, deferred work, frustration, and why electrical cannot be ignored.
📝 NoteThese are structured anchors, not an interrogation. Ask one question, shut up, listen, and capture their exact words. Later you will say: "Bob, earlier you told me…"
When to Move On Four signals you've built real rapport
  • They're providing good information beyond yes/no answers
  • You feel a genuine connection forming
  • They seem comfortable, open, and ready to engage
  • You've identified at least one emotional driver
🔑 Key Takeaway"Activate" means you have something specific to tie back to later. If you cannot write down at least one personal anchor in the customer's own words, you are not done.
Rapport Scorecard Grade yourself honestly after every visit

After every visit, grade yourself honestly. This is how you get better.

A
Activated & Connected — Found their emotional driver AND built personal/professional connection. Full rapport. The gold standard.
B
Activated Only — Found their emotional driver but didn't build the personal/professional connection. Still strong, but incomplete.
C
Asked F.O.R.M. — Went through the motions. Asked the questions but didn't activate or truly connect. Mechanical, not human.
D
No Rapport Built — Acted as a tool, not a human. Showed up, diagnosed, quoted. Zero connection. This is a problem.
⚠ ImportantGrade B or below means you're leaving money on the table — and more importantly, leaving a person feeling unheard. Aim for A every single visit.
