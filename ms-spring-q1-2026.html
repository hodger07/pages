Volume Story

Total Q1 demand fell ~16% year-over-year, yet cancellations dropped ~27% — meaning the team converted a higher share of what booked. Quality improved as volume contracted.

Geography Story

Houston-proper and Humble lost ~28 jobs each YoY. Spring held flat. Conroe emerged from 3 jobs to 13 (+333%). Center of gravity moved north toward Spring–Tomball–The Woodlands.

Warning Sign

"Found Another Company Sooner" nearly doubled (6 → 11) and is now the #2 cancellation reason. Speed-to-close is the issue — not service execution.

What This Means & What to Do
01
Demand is contracting but quality is holding — protect the operational discipline.
Volume −16%, completions −14%, cancels −27%. The team is converting better on a smaller funnel. Don't change what's working operationally while chasing volume recovery.
02
Lean marketing spend north — Conroe is a real opening.
Houston-proper and Humble each gave back ~28 jobs. Spring/Tomball/Woodlands held. Conroe went 3→13 with zero cancellations (+333%). The territory is telling you where to push.
03
Tighten quote-to-dispatch SLAs in inner-Houston ZIPs — competitive speed-to-close is the new leak.
"Found Another Company Sooner" doubled and is now #2. Target 77018, 77064, 77068 specifically — all spiked in cancel rate and have the highest competitive density.
04
Audit Spring dispatch and ETA confirmation — the core city is showing a warning sign.
Volume was essentially flat (179→175) but cancels went 23→26 against improving operations everywhere else. Something specific is happening in Spring. Investigate dispatch / ETA confirmation there.
05
Codify the upsell motion — fans/lights and safety checks are growing while overall volume falls.
Elective category grew 18% in a down market. Fans/Lights 20+ and Safety Upgrades 20+ both nearly doubled. Find out what the techs are doing in-home and put it in the playbook for the full roster.
