Stillforest Building Committee — For the Record

45 Stillforest Street: Written Record of Screening & Vegetation Restoration Commitments

Property line: 45 Stillforest (Fred Tavakoli) / 43 Stillforest (Richard Hodge)  ·  Compiled May 15, 2026
Purpose of this document: This page compiles, verbatim and in chronological order, the written commitments made by the architect of record, the HOA Building Committee, and the property owner at 45 Stillforest regarding restoration of the vegetative screen along the shared southern property line. Every statement below is sourced directly from emails already in the Committee's possession. No statements have been editorialized or embellished. The purpose is to assist the Committee in exercising the authority it already established in writing.

Chronological Record

Condition of Approval
HOA Building Committee — Vegetation Restoration Made a Formal Condition
Rod Proto · Stillforest Building Committee · April 27, 2025
Date: Sunday, April 27, 2025 — 9:43 AM CDT
From: Rod Proto <rod@protoenterprises.com>
To: Fred Tavakoli, Craig Stiteler, Jonathan Finger, Don Jones, Paul Parsons, Scott Epps, Jesse Blackwell
CC: Richard Hodge, C. Mitchell Cox
Subject: Re: Pool Plans
"Neighbor Consideration and Restoration of Vegetation: The existing chain-link fence on the adjoining property to your south is recessed approximately 18 inches within that neighbor's property line. During recent construction activities, vegetation located on this adjacent property along that fence line was removed without prior consultation or approval from the neighbor. As part of the Committee's conditions for approval, we respectfully request that the new fencing with columns and wrought iron installation include a live vegetative screen (such as shrubbery or hedges) along the southern boundary to replace the visual barrier that was previously in place with a similar look."
Significance: This is not a suggestion — it is stated as a "condition of approval" for the fence and pool submission. The Committee established this requirement in writing, copied to both parties. It has never been formally withdrawn or satisfied.
Condition of Approval
Rod Proto — Defined the Screening Standard
Rod Proto · Stillforest Building Committee · April 28, 2025
Date: Monday, April 28, 2025 — 6:05 PM CDT
From: Rod Proto <rod@protoenterprises.com>

What the Written Record Establishes

  • The Committee established a vegetative screening condition in writing on April 27, 2025 — as a condition of approval, not a request.
  • The architect of record confirmed in writing on February 4, 2025: "We will need to provide screening for sure."
  • The property owner committed in his own words to completing required landscaping per City and HOA requirements once construction reached the final phase — and copied the City on that commitment.
  • Landscaping approval was withheld by the Committee as far back as November 2023. It has never been granted.
  • No written objection to the screening condition was filed. The condition was accepted by conduct.
  • The Committee has both the authority and the written record to require compliance. The only question is whether that authority will be exercised.
