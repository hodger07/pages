§ 4

Distribution Strategy & Estimated Business Impact

Public Service Announcement Protocol

This campaign is classified as a Public Service Announcement distributing factual documentation. All timelines, BBB "F" ratings, and failure records are extracted directly from primary-source correspondence. The objective is informational: ensuring the professional trade community has access to verified post-sale liability data prior to specification.

The Target: B2B Specifiers

At a retail price point of $3,000–$10,000+, ThermaSol steam generators are not direct-to-consumer impulse buys. Revenue is driven almost entirely by B2B specification decisions made upstream of the homeowner. For this reason, the PSA campaign bypasses the consumer entirely and targets the entities who control the blueprints:

Custom Home Builders

Builders maintain curated "preferred brand" spec sheets. A ThermaSol specification here means recurring, uncontested revenue across dozens of homes per year. Builders are highly averse to post-sale liability.

Luxury Architects & Remodelers

Architects writing steam systems into master bath blueprints are the highest-leverage sales touchpoint in the industry. A single architect’s preference represents compounding multi-year revenue with zero incremental marketing cost to the manufacturer.

Automated Deployment Pipeline

The factual documentation is distributed through a two-channel automated pipeline deployed on a 30/60/90 day programmatic rollout schedule:

Programmatic Ad Buying

Geofencing 200 highly affluent zip codes, deploying targeted search and display ads autonomously. These ads intercept high-intent queries (e.g., "custom steam shower spec") and route traffic to the factual timeline.

CRM B2B Sequencing

Automated email sequencing delivering the factual timeline directly to the inboxes of local luxury builders and architects. No solicitation. Documentation only. Recipients are equipped to make informed liability decisions.

Estimated Business Impact: The "Spec Sheet" Collapse

When a builder or architect removes a brand from their preferred specification list because they refuse to carry the post-sale liability of an "F" rated company, the revenue consequence is not linear—it is compounding and multi-year.

  • Year 1 (Direct Loss): The builder replaces ThermaSol with a competitor for current projects. Revenue goes to zero.
  • Year 2 (Compounding Loss): The competitor gains installation history with the builder’s trades team. Familiarity builds preference. The switch cost has been paid.
  • Year 3+ (Permanent Displacement): The competitor is now the default in the standard specification package. ThermaSol is permanently locked out without a massive, proactive sales effort against an entrenched incumbent.
